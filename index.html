Common questions

My progress is gone. The game saves on your device only. Deleting the app deletes the save, and it does not transfer to a new phone.

I bought VIP and it is not showing. Open the Quests tab, scroll to the shop and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID that bought it.

Do dragons earn while the game is closed? No. Nothing is earned offline, on purpose.
