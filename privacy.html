Privacy Policy

Dragon Gambler · last updated 1 October 2026

The short version

Dragon Gambler collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours for anything to be sent to.

What stays on your device

Your game — gold, dragons, eggs, the codex, achievements and your sound setting — is written to a file inside the app on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple's services

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by the App Store. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only learns which items you own.

Game Center. If you are signed in, your total gold earned and the nickname you chose for Game Center are sent to Apple's leaderboard so that players can be ranked. This is optional: decline it, or turn Game Center off in Settings, and the rest of the game is unaffected.

Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
